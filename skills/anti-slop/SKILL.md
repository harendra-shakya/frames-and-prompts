# Anti-slop review

This is the **final quality gate**. Do not grade the cleverness of the prompt or the ambition of the plan. Inspect the artifact as a normal viewer or player would.

## Failure patterns: identify the symptom and fix it

| Slop symptom | Concrete fix |
|---|---|
| Generic "cinematic 8K masterpiece" look with no identity | Pick a specific composition, focal point, lighting setup, medium and visual grammar |
| Every shot is a symmetrical portrait or slow push-in | Rebuild shot coverage around dramatic beats and camera purpose |
| Excessive particle FX, bloom, grain, smoke or lens flare | Remove effects that hide the subject or reveal nothing |
| Random cuts/transitions used as excitement | Cut on action, information, emotional contrast, or a sound bridge |
| Character looks change between shots/panels | Lock reference sheets, distinguishing traits, costume and controlled source inputs |
| AI video morphs hands, faces, props or environmental geometry | Shorten/replace the faulty motion, isolate the action, edit/refine keyframes |
| "Animation" is an unchanged still with pan/zoom | Add actual pose/performance motion, or label and direct it honestly as a motion comic |
| Music overwhelms dialogue; missing singing, voice or effects | Build the sound plan early and review playback with audio, not mute previews |
| Beautiful panels but unclear action/reading order | Re-thumbnail the sequence and repair geography, continuity and gutter logic |
| Game concept art that fails as an asset/UI | Test at in-game size, correct silhouettes, pivots, state transitions, interaction clarity |
| Prompt overstuffed with contradictory styles or models | Reduce to one dominant medium/style and only the necessary controlled variables |
| Output looks polished at thumbnail size but breaks in detail | Inspect full resolution and targeted crops, then fix the actual errors |

## Hard gates

Do not approve a final artifact if any applicable gate fails:

1. **Brief fidelity:** all requested media, content, characters, style constraints, length, speech/music, interaction, and format are present.
2. **Meaning:** a viewer can follow the intended action or a player can understand what to do.
3. **Craft:** anatomy/poses, composition, motion, editing, material behavior, typography, performance, and audio meet the medium's expectations.
4. **Consistency:** identity, world, style, scale, geography, lighting and important props hold across connected assets.
5. **Technical playback:** exports open, resolution/aspect ratio are correct, audio works, animation plays, and UI/game inputs respond.
6. **Evidence:** someone actually viewed the final images or played the final timeline/build. No "verified" claims based only on source files or generated text.

When a render cannot be viewed or tested, mark the corresponding gate **unverified**, not passed.

## Review procedure

1. **Cold view.** Open the artifact without its prompt. Note what you actually see/hear and whether the intention is legible.
2. **Compare.** Put the artifact beside 1–3 selected references and the brief. Compare specific observable properties, not the prestige of named artists or tools.
3. **Find the top defect.** Identify the most damaging moment or component and why it damages the audience experience.
4. **Targeted repair.** Fix that defect with the smallest change that preserves successful work.
5. **Recheck.** Review the repaired segment **and** adjacent continuity/entire composition. If it is a film, play beginning to end with sound. If a game, play the critical loop.
6. **Stop consciously.** Stop after applicable gates pass and further changes have lower expected value or introduce more risk than benefit. There is no mandatory number of revisions.

## How to evaluate

Use a compact review record, not a self-congratulatory score:

| Criterion | Pass / fail / unverified | Evidence or defect |
|---|---|---|
| Brief fidelity | | |
| Story or gameplay clarity | | |
| Visual craft | | |
| Motion/audio (if applicable) | | |
| Continuity | | |
| Export/playback | | |

A claimed "10/10" is not evidence. Good work holds up at normal viewing size, through transitions, in motion and with real controls. Name remaining limitations plainly.

## Avoid presentation slop too

Say what the work is, what you inspected, and what remains. No decorative hype, unsupported adjectives, fake awards, invented reference analysis, or screenshots substituted for a requested video. Strong direction and a verified result are more valuable than a long prompt.
